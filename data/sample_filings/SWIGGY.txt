PROSPECTUS CHAPTER EXTRACTION: SWIGGY LIMITED (DRHP / RHP)
================================================================================

[SECTION: OBJECTS OF THE ISSUE & USE OF PROCEEDS]
The Offer comprises a Fresh Issue of Equity Shares aggregating up to ₹4,499.00 Crores and an Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 175,087,863 Equity Shares aggregating up to ₹6,828.43 Crores by the Selling Shareholders. Total Issue Size is ₹11,327.43 Crores at the upper price band of ₹390 per share.
OFS represents approximately 60.28% of the total issue size. The Selling Shareholders include early venture capital investors (Prosus/Naspers, SoftBank, Accel, Elevation Capital, Tencent) and certain individual promoter/executive shareholders.
The Net Proceeds from the Fresh Issue are proposed to be utilized as follows:
1. Investment in Material Subsidiary, Scootsy Logistics Private Limited, for expansion of Dark Store network (Instamart): ₹1,178.70 Crores.
2. Investment in technology and cloud infrastructure: ₹703.40 Crores.
3. Brand marketing and business promotion expenses for enhancing brand awareness: ₹1,115.30 Crores.
4. Repayment or pre-payment, in full or part, of certain borrowings availed by Scootsy: ₹137.50 Crores.
5. Funding inorganic growth through acquisitions and general corporate purposes: ₹964.10 Crores.
None of the Fresh Issue proceeds will be utilized for payments to founders or executive directors except for legitimate compensation or reimbursement of verified operational expenses.

[SECTION: RISK FACTORS]
1. History of Net Losses: We have incurred net losses in each year since our incorporation. For FY22, FY23, and FY24, our restated loss for the year was ₹3,628.90 Cr, ₹4,179.30 Cr, and ₹2,350.24 Cr respectively. If we are unable to generate sufficient revenue and achieve unit economic profitability across quick commerce and food delivery, our business, cash flows, and financial condition will continue to suffer.
2. Negative Cash Flows from Operations: We experienced negative cash flows from operating activities of ₹2,284.15 Cr in FY22, ₹3,568.20 Cr in FY23, and ₹1,132.80 Cr in FY24. We anticipate operating cash outflows to persist in the near term as we aggressively expand quick-commerce dark store infrastructure.
3. Intense Industry Competition: We face fierce competition from incumbent delivery platforms (principally Zomato / Blinkit) as well as emerging rapid delivery operators (Zepto) and quick-commerce arms of established e-commerce players (Tata BigBasket, Amazon, Flipkart Minutes). Increased discount wars and driver incentive subsidies may permanently erode take rates and EBITDA margins.
4. Customer and Gig Worker Retention: High churn among delivery partners (gig workers) and rising fuel/labor costs could inflate delivery costs per order. Regulatory interventions demanding statutory social security benefits for gig workers under the Code on Social Security could materially increase operational overheads.
5. Dark Store Lease Commitments and Urban Rent Inflation: Quick commerce viability depends on securing prime micro-warehousing spaces in dense tier-1 metropolitan neighborhoods at viable lease rates.

Promoter Classification: Under SEBI ICDR regulations, the company is a professionally managed company without an identifiable institutional promoter; therefore, no promoter group shares are subject to promoter lock-in; however, key pre-IPO investors are subject to the standard statutory 6-month pre-issue lock-in.

[SECTION: RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS]
During FY24, related party transactions totaled ₹342.10 Crores, consisting of:
- Logistics service payments to Scootsy Logistics Private Limited (wholly owned subsidiary).
- Cloud and technical service fees paid to entities affiliated with board investors (Tencent Cloud / AWS affiliate programs) at arm's-length commercial terms.
- Lease payments for corporate offices to holding entities amounting to ₹18.40 Crores.
- No uncollateralized loans or interest-free advances were extended to executive directors or key management relatives.

[SECTION: OUTSTANDING LITIGATIONS AND DEFAULTS]
1. Tax Demands: Total disputed indirect tax (GST and Service Tax) and direct tax demands against the Company aggregate to ₹412.30 Crores. This includes a show-cause notice from the Directorate General of GST Intelligence (DGGI) regarding the classification of delivery fees collected on behalf of restaurant partners.
2. Consumer Disputes: 1,240 consumer forum cases pending across various district and state consumer forums regarding order delays, deficient food quality, and refund claims, involving aggregate claims of ₹14.80 Crores.
3. CCI Anti-Trust Investigation: The Competition Commission of India (CCI) has an ongoing probe into alleged anti-competitive conduct, bundling of services, and deep discounting practices in the online food delivery sector involving both Swiggy and Zomato.
Contingent liabilities not provided for as of March 31, 2024 total ₹438.10 Crores, representing 4.8% of consolidated net worth.
